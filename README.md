# ChemistryRecall

**ChemistryRecall** is a study-first chemistry practice app designed to help you revise concepts through focused Study Packs, adaptive practice, and fast recall.

---

## What ChemistryRecall Does

ChemistryRecall turns chemistry revision into short, repeatable practice sessions.

Instead of making you work through large chapter lists every time, you can choose a **Study Pack**, select how many questions you want to attempt, and start practising immediately.

The app keeps track of your progress locally so your revision history and progression continue across sessions.

---

## Study Packs

Study Packs are the main way to practise in ChemistryRecall.

You can choose from the available packs and start a session with:

- **10 questions**
- **25 questions**
- **50 questions**
- **100 questions**
- **Custom** question count
- **Endless Mode**

### Adaptive Practice

ChemistryRecall does not simply show questions in a fixed order.

Practice adapts to your performance by prioritising:

1. Questions you have **not seen yet**
2. Questions you have **previously missed**
3. Questions you are **weaker at and need to reinforce**

This helps you spend more time on the material that actually needs revision.

---

## Your Progress

ChemistryRecall keeps your study progression available between sessions.

Your progress includes:

- **XP**
- **Level progression**
- **Streaks**
- **Combos**
- **Daily Mission**
- **Achievements**
- **Recent Sessions**

These systems are designed to make regular revision easier to maintain without taking attention away from the chemistry itself.

---

## Class XI & Class XII

ChemistryRecall keeps **Class XI and Class XII** as separate study areas.

Choose the class you are currently studying to keep your revision focused on the appropriate material.

---

## Biomolecules — Class XII

Class XII includes dedicated Biomolecules recall practice with five modes:

### 1. Structure → Name
Identify a compound from its displayed structure.

### 2. Name → Structure
Recall and identify the structure corresponding to a compound's name.

### 3. Mixed Amino Acid Practice
Practise amino-acid identification and recall in a mixed format.

### 4. Vitamins Learner
Learn and reinforce vitamin-related information through guided recall.

### 5. Vitamins MCQ
Test your vitamin knowledge using multiple-choice questions.

---

## Chemistry Rendering

ChemistryRecall supports rich chemistry content directly inside questions and answers.

Depending on the study material, you may encounter:

- Chemical structures
- Molecular diagrams
- Reaction schemes
- Reaction arrows
- Reagents and conditions
- Inline chemical structures
- More complex chemistry layouts

The app renders chemical structures as vector graphics where possible, keeping them sharp when displayed at different sizes.

### Reliable Rendering

ChemistryRecall includes a built-in fallback renderer for chemistry structures.

This means chemistry content can remain visible even when the primary structure-rendering component is unavailable.

## Working With Questions

A typical study session is simple:

**Choose a class → Choose a Study Pack → Choose your session size → Start practising → Answer questions → Review your progress**

The goal is to keep the process fast so that you can spend your time **recalling chemistry rather than navigating the app**.

---

## Chapterwise Quizzes

ChemistryRecall currently focuses on **Study Packs** rather than a separate chapterwise quiz system.

The chapterwise quiz interface is therefore not part of the active app experience.

---

## Progress & Data

Your progression data is stored **locally on your device**.

This includes your XP, levels, streaks, achievements, combos, daily mission progress, and recent sessions.

If you use the app on another device, your locally stored progression may not automatically appear there.

---

## Tips for Better Revision

For the best results:

- Use **smaller sessions** when you are short on time.
- Use **25–50 questions** for regular revision.
- Use **100 questions** for longer practice sessions.
- Use **Endless Mode** when you want an extended recall session.
- Return to missed and weaker questions rather than only repeating questions you already know.
- Keep your streak going with consistent daily practice.
- Use the Biomolecules modes to practise both recognition and active recall.

---

## The Core Idea

ChemistryRecall is built around one simple principle:

> **See less. Recall more.**

The app is designed to make chemistry revision quick, focused, and repeatable — so you can spend more time actively retrieving information and less time managing your study material.
